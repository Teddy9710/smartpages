Maker first comment:

Hi Product Hunt — I built SmartPages to remove the repetitive work between completing a browser workflow and documenting it.

The idea is simple: record the workflow once, keep the useful steps and screenshots, then generate a structured draft with the model provider you already use. You remain in control of the provider, model, Base URL, editing, and export format.

SmartPages is ready to try as a Chrome/Edge extension. I am looking for feedback from product teams, QA engineers, support teams, and developers who regularly write guides, SOPs, test cases, or bug reproduction steps.
